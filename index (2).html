About us

Rooted in the land

Old MacDonald Farms deals in agricultural land, farmlands and managed farms, and takes projects from raw land through to development, construction and hospitality.

Based in Bangalore, Karnataka, with headquarters in both Bangalore and Munich, Germany, we bring local knowledge of Indian land together with an international outlook.
